### Global War Impact Analysis — Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Overview

A comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis of a 10,000-row global war dataset. The study examines economic and social indicators — GDP, inflation, poverty, unemployment, costs, and informal economy — to explore how conflicts disrupt economies, identify recovery challenges, and uncover statistical relationships between war severity and economic decline.

---

## Key Findings

| Indicator | Finding |
|---|---|
| GDP Decline | Wars consistently show sharp drops in GDP; prolonged conflicts link to slower recovery |
| Inflation Spikes | Inflation rises significantly during conflict periods, reflecting market instability |
| Unemployment & Poverty | Both increase during wars; poverty often persists long after conflict ends |
| Conflict Severity | Longer, costlier wars correlate with deeper economic collapse |
| Regional Variation | Some regions recover faster; others show prolonged instability across multiple indicators |

---

## Technical Workflow

**1. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing**
- Standardized fields: GDP, inflation, poverty, unemployment, costs, informal economy
- Verified data integrity and handled missing values

**2. Statistical Analysis**
- Generated descriptive statistics to study distributions and detect anomalies
- Applied correlation analysis to identify relationships between indicators

**3. Data Visualization**
- Bar charts — compared GDP decline and unemployment across regions
- Scatterplots & bubble charts — explored inflation, poverty, and war severity relationships
- Trend analysis — highlighted pre- vs. post-war economic shifts
